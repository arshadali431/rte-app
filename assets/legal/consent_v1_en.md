## 2. What we collect
Parent/guardian name and mobile number; child's name, date of birth and gender; Aadhaar number; photos of documents (such as Aadhaar, birth certificate, PAN and signature); RTE application details and status.

## 3. Why we collect it (purpose)
Only to (a) give you a token, (b) help you with your child's RTE admission application, (c) check your application status on the official portal, and (d) keep the Trust's records of this assistance, for verification and dispute resolution. We do not use this data for fundraising, advertising, events, or any other communication, and we do not track or profile children.

## 4. Your authorisation
You authorise the Trust's volunteers to fill in and submit your child's RTE application on your behalf using the information and documents you provide. You confirm that you are the parent or lawful guardian of the child and that the information you give is correct. Please verify your details before final submission. Entries are made from the information you give us.

## 5. Who can see your data
Only Trust volunteers and staff authorised for this work, who are bound by confidentiality. Data is stored on Google Firebase / Google Cloud services. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires.
